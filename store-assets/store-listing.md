## Full description
Bingo Night turns bingo into something you can play together from separate
phones — or solo, whenever you want a quick round.

HOW IT WORKS
Pick how you want to play: solo against a computer opponent, head-to-head
with one other player, or a party of up to four. Create a room, share the
code or QR link, and you're in.

AUTO-CALLER, NO ONE HAS TO HOST
Numbers call themselves automatically every 15 seconds — nobody needs to
run the game. Just watch, listen, and mark your own card.

REAL SYNC, NOT A GUESS
Every device in the room sees the same calls at the same time.

FIVE WAYS TO WIN
Choose your pattern each round: any line, four corners, postage stamp,
letter X, or a full blackout.

PLAY YOUR WAY
- Solo vs. Computer — quick practice, no partner needed
- Head to Head — just the two of you
- Party — up to four people competing at once

No accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases — just bingo.
