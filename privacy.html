Privacy Policy

Bingo Night — last updated September 2026

Bingo Night is a simple bingo game for playing with people you know. This page explains, plainly, what happens with your information when you use it.

What we collect

  • The name you type in. When you join a room, the display name you enter is stored with that room's game state so other players in the room can see who's playing. It isn't linked to an email, phone number, or account — there is no account system.
  • Game state. The room code, the numbers called, each player's card, and their marks are stored so the game can sync between devices. This is tied only to the room code, not to any personal identity.

What we don't collect

  • No accounts, sign-in, email addresses, or phone numbers
  • No location data
  • No advertising identifiers, and no ads shown in the app
  • No analytics or usage tracking

Where it's stored

Room and game data is stored in a database (Upstash Redis) accessed only through our own server-side code — never directly by your browser. Your device also keeps a small local record (your name, your last room code, and your sound preference) so returning to the app remembers your session; this stays on your device and is not sent anywhere except as part of normal gameplay.

Third parties

One feature relies on an outside service: the invite QR code is generated by api.qrserver.com, which receives only the room's join link (a URL containing a short room code) — nothing else. The app itself is hosted on Vercel.

How long data is kept

Room data persists until the room is naturally superseded (a new round or a new room) — there's currently no automatic deletion schedule. Room codes are short and not designed to keep anything sensitive private: don't put personal or sensitive information into your display name.

Children

Bingo Night isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age, beyond the display name typed into the app for gameplay purposes.
